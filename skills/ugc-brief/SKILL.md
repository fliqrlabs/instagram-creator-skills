# ugc-brief

Bad briefs produce 20 identical "OMG I love this" videos. Good briefs give creators a clear truth and room to say it their own way. That's what makes UGC convert. This skill writes the good kind, and adds a way to measure it beyond views.

Read: `../instagram-playbook/hooks.md`, `../instagram-playbook/safety.md` (disclosure, claims).

## The brief (one page per creator type)

```
CAMPAIGN      {name}  ·  goal: awareness / sales / app installs / leads  ·  live {dates}
PRODUCT TRUTH the one thing it does better, in a sentence a customer would say
              + 3 proof points (real: specs, results, reviews the brand can back up)
AUDIENCE      who's buying, their problem, the words they use
HOOK ANGLES   5 options, different formulas (problem/solution, before/after, honest review,
              "things I wish I knew", POV). Creators pick one or bring their own.
MUST SAY      product name · the one key benefit · the offer/code · the CTA
NEVER SAY     unapproved claims (medical, "guaranteed", "best in India"), competitor names, prices that change
SHOT LIST     product in hand in the first 3s · in use · the result · the face for the CTA
SPECS         9:16, 15-45s, burned-in captions, original audio or licensed music, safe zones respected
DELIVERABLES  {n} reels + {n} story frames · raw files Y/N · {n} revisions
USAGE RIGHTS  organic repost / paid ads (whitelisting) · duration (e.g. 3 months) · regions. Priced separately.
DISCLOSURE    Paid partnership label + #ad, as required (ASCI in India, FTC in the US)
CTA + FUNNEL  "Comment {KEYWORD} for {offer}". Each creator gets their own keyword or code so results can be attributed.
TIMELINE      brief → script approval → shoot → draft → revisions → post date
```

## The funnel (the part most briefs miss)

Give each creator a **unique keyword or code** (GLOW1, GLOW2…). On the creator's account, a `comment-to-dm` automation delivers the brand's offer link and code. The creator approves it on their account, created paused and then activated. Afterwards, compare `total_triggers` per creator with sales per code. That shows which creator actually drove buyers.

## Writing rules

- Give creators **angles, not scripts**. A word-for-word script reads like an ad. Exceptions: compliance lines and the CTA wording.
- Every product claim must be one the brand can substantiate. Flag anything regulated (skincare results, supplements, finance).
- Hook angles must fit the creator's voice. Ask for the creator list, and tailor 1-2 angles per creator if there are fewer than 10.

## Creator side

If a **creator** asks for this skill about a brand deal: write the questions to send the brand (usage rights, whitelisting, exclusivity, payment terms, revisions, disclosure), and then the brief back to them in this format. Pair it with `brand-pitch` for the rates.
